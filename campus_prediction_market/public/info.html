UNDERSTANDING MARKETS

What is a prediction market?

A prediction market is a market built around a question about a future event. Participants consider possible outcomes and the market reflects the collective expectations of its participants.

A question with possible outcomes

Each market is centred on a clearly defined event or question.

Market expectations

Market prices or displayed probabilities can change as expectations change.

Resolution

The market is resolved according to its published rules and verified outcome.
