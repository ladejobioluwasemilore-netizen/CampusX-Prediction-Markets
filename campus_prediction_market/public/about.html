OUR STORY

Built around the
campus experience.

Campus life is full of moments worth paying attention to. From sports and campus events to student leadership, student union affairs and conversations that shape everyday university life.

CampusX is designed to give these moments a place. We bring together a platform where students can explore campus-related markets, follow developments and engage with the conversations that matter to their communities.

We believe campus experiences deserve a platform that reflects the energy, opinions and perspectives of the people who live them.
WHY WE EXIST

Our purpose. Our direction.

CampusX is built around a simple idea: campus life is more interesting when people can follow it, understand it and participate in the conversation.

Our mission

To create a connected campus platform where students can explore prediction markets, engage with campus developments and share perspectives through one accessible experience.

Our vision

To become a recognised destination for campus-focused conversations and prediction markets, connecting student communities through information, participation and shared experiences.
